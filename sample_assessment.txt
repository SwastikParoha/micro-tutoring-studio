Diagnostic demo lesson notes — 45 min, maths

Pupil: Ava Patel, Year 3 (age 8). Really into pizza, football (supports the local team),
and Minecraft. Mum's name is Priya, happy to be contacted by WhatsApp.

Started with a warm-up on times tables — Ava was quick and confident on 2s, 5s and 10s,
got most of the 3s. Number bonds to 20 were fluent.

Fractions were the weak area:
- When I showed 1/2 and 1/4 she said 1/4 was bigger "because 4 is more than 2". Classic
  misconception — she's using whole-number thinking on the denominator.
- She could shade "one part" of a shape but didn't always check the parts were equal.
- Struggled to find 1/4 of 12 counters — didn't connect it to sharing/dividing.
- Couldn't write "three quarters" as 3/4, wrote "34".

Reading a fraction diagram: OK when the shape was already split equally, lost when asked
to split it herself.

Overall she got about 6/15 on the fractions check. Lovely attitude, kept trying, responds
really well to drawing things out. Would benefit from lots of visual/pizza-based work.
